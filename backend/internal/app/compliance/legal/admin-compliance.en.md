# AirGate Deployment and Operation Compliance Commitment (v2026.06.15)

> This commitment applies to any party that deploys, configures, or operates this system (AirGate, the "System", and such party, the "Operator"). Please read and acknowledge each item before using the System as an administrator.

## 1. Scope

This commitment applies to any individual or organization that deploys, hosts, resells, or otherwise makes the System's capabilities available to others. Whether for personal use or for providing services to others, the Operator must independently ensure its conduct is lawful and compliant.

## 2. Responsibility of the Operator

1. The Operator shall **independently assess and bear** all legal responsibility for its deployment and operation within every applicable jurisdiction.
2. The Operator shall independently ensure it has obtained the **authorizations, qualifications, and filings** required for its business, and holds lawful rights to use the accounts and credentials of upstream providers (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, etc.).
3. The Operator shall not transfer its responsibilities, in any manner, to the System's open-source project, its authors, or contributors.

1. The System is open-source software. Its authors and contributors have **no affiliation, partnership, or agency relationship** with any third-party deployment or operation.
2. The Operator independently bears all consequences arising from its deployment, including but not limited to data security, content compliance, billing and refund disputes, and upstream account bans or restrictions.
3. The Operator shall **not** imply or claim any endorsement, authorization, or warranty from the System's open-source project.

## 4. Compliance Commitments

As the Operator, I commit to:

1. **Comply with upstream terms**: comply with the service agreements and usage policies of the upstream AI providers I connect to, and not circumvent their regional, account, or risk-control restrictions.
2. **Lawful credentials**: all configured upstream accounts and API keys are lawfully held, and were not obtained through theft, fraud, or in violation of any agreement.
3. **Lawful provision of services**: not provide resale or agency services to the general public without the necessary authorization and qualifications; where operating within mainland China, independently complete the required filings and compliance assessments.
4. **User management mechanisms**: establish user onboarding, content moderation, and abuse-handling mechanisms commensurate with the business risk.
5. **No misrepresentation**: not make false or misleading statements to users or third parties regarding the source, capabilities, or compliance status of the service.
6. **Independent liability**: independently bear all legal, financial, and reputational consequences arising from operating the System.
